# Backlog

Ideas deliberately excluded from v1. Recorded rather than absorbed, so that scope
stays honest and the reasoning survives.

## Candidate extensions

- Extend the peer set beyond eight companies
- Add non-European reference points to show best-in-class globally
- Score across multiple years to show trajectory rather than a snapshot
- Financial modelling of the reinvention opportunity
- AX design specifications for the remaining opportunity areas
- Sensitivity analysis on the effect of removing any single dimension

## Deliberately not planned

- Primary research, interviews or surveys. Changes the nature of the study and
  the claims it can support
- Predictive or machine-learning scoring. The value here is a transparent rubric,
  not an opaque model
- Claims about internal capability. Outside what public disclosure can support
