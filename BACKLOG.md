# Backlog

Ideas deliberately excluded from v1. Recorded rather than absorbed, so that scope
stays honest and the reasoning survives.

## Framework refinements for v2

**Governance anchors: missing rung for adjacent-domain ownership.**
Surfaced during the GEA assessment. The anchors distinguish "no owner" (0 to 2)
from "a named accountable role for AI" (3), but have no rung for the common
middle case: an executive in an adjacent domain, typically a Chief Digital or
Chief Information Officer, whose remit demonstrably includes AI without any
disclosure stating accountability for it. GEA sits exactly there and scores 1,
which is defensible under the anchors as written but coarser than the evidence
warrants.

Not amended in v1. Changing anchors after companies have been scored would
invalidate completed assessments and destroy the rubric-precedes-data property.
The correct time to fix this is a deliberate v2 with a full re-score.

## Deliberately not planned

- Primary research, interviews or surveys. Changes the nature of the study and
  the claims it can support
- Predictive or machine-learning scoring. The value here is a transparent rubric,
  not an opaque model
- Claims about internal capability. Outside what public disclosure can support
